What it does
  • Tasks List, board, and calendar views. Filter by status or tag. Tasks with due dates sit alongside your calendar events, no context switching.
  • Board 🌑 To Do · 🌓 In Progress · 🌕 Done. Drag to move, swipe to complete.
  • Calendar Connects to Apple Calendar, Google, Outlook. Any account synced to your iPhone. Tap a day to see tasks and events together.
  • Notes vault Every task links to a .md file in a folder you choose. Open in Obsidian, iA Writer, or any text editor. No lock-in.
  • Tags Health · Personal · Learning · Social · Finance · Work. Colour-coded, filterable, yours to customise.
  • Platforms iPhone and Mac. One app, not two.
