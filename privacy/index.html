Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Labyrinth has no server. Your tasks and notes sit in a folder you chose, on a device you own.

What is collected

Nothing. Labyrinth has no account system, no user profile, and no backend. Your tasks, notes, and calendar data stay on your device unless you move them yourself.

Where your data lives

Tasks are stored locally using CoreData. Notes are saved as plain .md files in a folder you choose. Both can be opened outside the app in any text editor or file manager.

Calendar access

Labyrinth asks for access to your calendar so it can show events alongside your tasks. Access is read-only. Nothing is copied off your device.

Problem reports

If you shake your device to report a problem, Labyrinth sends what you write, plus a screenshot of the current screen, to a private issue tracker so the problem can be fixed. This only happens when you choose to send a report. You can see the screenshot before it goes. If the screen shows something you would rather keep private, cancel and report it another way.

Analytics and tracking

None. No analytics tools, no advertising identifiers, no third-party tracking.

Crash reports

If you share diagnostics with Apple in your device settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash logs to developers through Xcode. That is controlled by your settings, not by Labyrinth, and your identity is never included.

Children

Labyrinth is rated 4+ and safe for all ages. No data is collected from any user, at any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it. Nothing will start being collected without being written here first.
