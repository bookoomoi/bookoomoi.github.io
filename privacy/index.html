Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Labyrinth collects no personal data. Everything stays on your device. Most apps need two screens of SDKs to tell you what they track. Labyrinth needs none.

What Labyrinth collects

Nothing. Labyrinth does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any server. Tasks, notes, and calendar data never leave your device unless you choose to move them.

Data storage

Task data is stored locally using CoreData. Notes are stored as plain .md files in a folder you choose, readable in any text editor or file manager outside the app.

Calendar access

Labyrinth requests read-only access to Apple Calendar to display events alongside tasks. Calendar data is never transmitted anywhere.

Analytics and tracking

None. No analytics SDKs. No advertising identifiers. No crash reporting tied to your identity. No third-party tracking of any kind.

Crash reports

If you have opted into sharing diagnostics with Apple through iOS Settings, Apple may collect anonymous crash logs visible to developers through Xcode Organizer. This is controlled by your device settings, not by Labyrinth. No identity is ever visible.

In-app purchases

Pro subscription payments are processed entirely by Apple. Labyrinth never sees or stores payment information.

Children

Labyrinth is rated 4+ and collects no data from any user regardless of age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page updates. Data collection will never be introduced without stating it here first.
