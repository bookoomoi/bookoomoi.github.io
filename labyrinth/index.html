What it does
  • Tasks List, board, and calendar views. Filter by status or tag. Tasks with due dates sit next to your calendar events.
  • Board 🌑 To Do · 🌓 In Progress · 🌕 Done. Drag to move, swipe to complete.
  • Calendar Reads any calendar already synced to your device. Apple, Google, Outlook. Tap a day for tasks and events together.
  • Notes vault Every task links to a .md file in a folder you pick. Open it in Obsidian, iA Writer, or anything that reads text.
  • Tags Health · Personal · Learning · Social · Finance · Work. Add your own. Filter any view.
  • Platforms iPhone and Mac. Runs on iOS 17 and macOS 14 or later; check the App Store for the current requirement. Widgets, Shortcuts, and Siri to come.

No data collected.
No cloud required.

  • Everything stays on your device
  • Plain .md files, open and readable across text editors
  • No account required, analytics, or advertising identifiers
